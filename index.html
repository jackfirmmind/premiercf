How we sell a business

Five stages. You stay focused on running the company while we manage the buyers, the lawyers and the timetable.

  1. Valuation and readiness

    We tell you what the business is worth today and what would increase its value.

  2. Preparation

    We prepare the sale document, the financial information and the data room.

  3. Buyer approach

    We contact suitable trade and private equity buyers in the UK and abroad, in confidence.

  4. Offers and negotiation

    We create competition between buyers, compare the offers, and agree heads of terms.

  5. Due diligence and completion

    We manage the buyer's checks and the lawyers through to completion.
