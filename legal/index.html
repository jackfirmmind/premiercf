What we collect

When you contact us or use a form on this website, we collect:

  • your name;
  • your email address and phone number;
  • your company name;
  • anything else you choose to tell us in your message.

If you become a client, we also collect the information we need to carry out the work and to meet our legal duties.

How we use it

We only use your information when the law allows it. The table shows each use and the legal reason for it.

How we use your information and the legal basis
What we doLegal basis
Reply to your enquiry and arrange a call.Our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for automated decisions about you.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to. This can include:

  • companies that run services for us, such as website hosting, form handling and email;
  • our professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants;
  • regulators or law enforcement, when the law requires it.

Some of these companies store data outside the UK. When they do, we make sure there are proper safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 2 years if you do not become a client. We keep client records for 6 years after our work ends, for legal and tax reasons. After that, we delete them securely.

We keep your information on password-protected systems. Only people who need it can see it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it;
  • ask us to send your information to another organisation;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

These requests are free. We reply within 1 month. To make a request, email brendan@premiercf.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would like the chance to fix the problem first, so please contact us.

Cookies
