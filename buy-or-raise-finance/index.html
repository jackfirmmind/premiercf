Buy a business

The right acquisition adds services, accreditations, people or locations faster than you could build them. We find the targets and run the deal for you.

Discuss an acquisition

How we run an acquisition

  1. Agree what to buy

    We agree the type, size, location and price range of business that fits your plan.

  2. Find and approach targets

    We research the market and approach owners directly and in confidence. Many good targets are not for sale until someone asks.

  3. Value and make an offer

    We value each target, structure the offer and negotiate heads of terms.

  4. Due diligence and completion

    We manage the checks, the lawyers and the funding through to completion.

Management buy-outs and buy-ins

In a management buy-out, the people who run the business buy it from the owner. In a buy-in, an outside team buys it. Both need a fair price, the right structure and the right funding.

Discuss a buy-out

For the owner

A sale to people you know and trust. The business keeps its name, its staff and its customers.

For the management team

Ownership of the business you already run. We help you raise the money and agree fair terms.

What we do

  • Value the business and agree a fair price for both sides.
  • Structure the deal. This can include bank debt, private equity, deferred payments and loan notes from the owner.
  • Prepare the business plan and raise the funding from banks and investors.
  • Negotiate the terms and manage the deal through to completion.

Release equity without selling everything

You can take some value out of the business now and keep control. This suits owners who want financial security but are not ready to leave.

Discuss equity release

  • Sell a minority stake

    An investor buys part of your shares. You receive cash now and keep running the business.

  • Refinance the business

    The business borrows against its value and pays part of that money to the shareholders.

  • Sell to an employee ownership trust

    The business is sold to a trust that holds it for the staff. The owner is paid over time from profits.

  • Sell in stages

    Sell a majority now and the rest later, often at a higher value as the business grows.
