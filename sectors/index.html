Testing, inspection and certification

Laboratories, inspection firms and certification bodies check that products, assets and sites meet the required standards. Most of these firms are owner-managed. Large groups and private equity-backed platforms buy them often.

In the last 3 years there have been more than 280 deals in the UK TIC sector, by more than 30 different buyers. Some buyers complete a deal every month.

We know these buyers, what they look for, and what they pay. That knowledge helps us find the right buyer and get the best terms for you.
