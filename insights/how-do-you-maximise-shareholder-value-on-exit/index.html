How to get the best price on exit

The best price comes from competition between buyers who have the cash to pay it.

By Premier Corporate Finance

In this guide

  1. The right buyer at the right time
  2. Talk to the right buyers
  3. Approach them at the right time
  4. Next steps

The right buyer at the right time

The right buyer

A buyer with the cash to pay the highest value.

The right time

The moment when a buyer's appetite matches yours.

Most shareholders release the real value of their shares through a full or partial sale. You get the most value when you are ready for a sale, and connected to the right buyer at the right time.

The challenge is finding that buyer in a market that changes every day, and finding them at the right moment.

1 Talk to the right buyers

Talking to the wrong buyers wastes a lot of time. Profile each buyer first. Check that they have the funds, that they fit your business, and that they complete the deals they start. Then shortlist the right ones.

2 Approach them at the right time

Sell when the business is performing at its best. Be ready before you start. Approach buyers when they are in buying mode.

Premier follows the buyers in your sector, their track record and their appetite, so you approach the right ones at the right time.
