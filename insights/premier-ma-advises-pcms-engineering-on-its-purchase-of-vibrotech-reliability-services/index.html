Deal announcement

Premier advises PCMS Engineering on its purchase of Vibrotech Reliability Services

Premier advised PCMS Engineering, a leading condition monitoring business, on its purchase of Vibrotech Reliability Services.

About the deal

Vibrotech was founded in 1997. It provides predictive maintenance services, mainly condition monitoring, from its base in Stokesley, Middlesbrough.

The purchase also added value for Vibrotech's oil analysis customers, because PCMS runs one of only a few independent oil laboratories in the UK. Rupert Lewis led the deal.
