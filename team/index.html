How we work

Four commitments we make to every client, whatever the size of the deal.

  • A partner leads your deal

    You deal with the same senior person from the first meeting to completion. We do not pass your deal to a junior team.

  • We know your sector

    We have worked on deals across testing, inspection and related services for many years. We know the buyers and what they pay for.

  • Confidential from day one

    Buyers see an anonymous profile first and sign a confidentiality agreement before they learn your name.

  • We act only for you

    We are paid to get the best outcome for our client. We do not act for the other side in the same deal.
